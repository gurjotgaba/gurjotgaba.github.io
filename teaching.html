Teaching profile

Active, applied, and research-informed learning

I teach cybersecurity, information security, cryptography, network security, and computer networks across undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional education. My teaching emphasises active learning, practical security analysis, real-world cases, responsible decision-making, and alignment with contemporary standards and research.

I also contribute to curriculum design, educational videos, project-based assessment, laboratories, Capture-the-Flag activities, and continuing development of cybersecurity education for business professionals.
