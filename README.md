# Gurjot Singh Gaba | Academic & Research Website

Personal academic website of **Dr Gurjot Singh Gaba**, Assistant Professor of Cybersecurity at **Linköping University, Sweden**, and part-time Researcher at **Kaunas University of Technology, Lithuania**.

🌐 **Website:** https://gurjotgaba.github.io/

## About

This website provides an overview of my academic career, research contributions, teaching activities, professional engagements, and international collaborations.

My research focuses on cybersecurity, with particular interests in applied cryptography, secure communication protocols, authentication, network security, cyber risk assessment, and post-quantum security. I am especially interested in developing and evaluating security solutions for aviation systems, critical infrastructure, and emerging digital technologies.

## Research and Collaboration

My work involves interdisciplinary and international collaboration with academic institutions, industry partners, and research organisations. Through these collaborations, I aim to contribute to the development of secure, resilient, and trustworthy digital systems.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration, joint publications, student supervision, and participation in collaborative research initiatives.
