Research

Research themes

Security and resilience across emerging networks, connected systems, and critical infrastructures.

01

Applied Cryptography

Design and analysis of authentication, key agreement, access control, privacy-preserving, and secure handover mechanisms for IoT, industrial, healthcare, transportation, and communication systems.

Authentication · Key exchange · Zero-knowledge proofs · Privacy · Secure handover

02

Post-Quantum and Quantum-Ready Security

Post-quantum migration, crypto-agility, quantum readiness assessment, QKD, EuroQCI, hybrid key establishment, and governance frameworks for the transition to quantum-resilient infrastructures.

PQC · QKD · EuroQCI · Crypto-agility · Readiness assessment

03

Aviation Cybersecurity

Security of CPDLC, LDACS, future aviation communication infrastructures, air traffic management, cyber risk assessment, attack simulation, and stakeholder awareness.

CPDLC · LDACS · ATM · SecRAM · Cyber-awareness

04

Industrial and IoT Security

Security and privacy in Industry 4.0 and 5.0, IT/OT convergence, industrial communication systems, cyber-physical systems, IoT, IIoT, and Internet of Medical Things environments.

Industry 4.0 · Industry 5.0 · IoT · IIoT · IT/OT

05

Cyber Resilience and Critical Infrastructure

Cyber risk assessment, digital sovereignty, zero trust, secure communications, organisational readiness, and resilience across aviation, energy, maritime, and industrial infrastructures.

Critical infrastructure · Zero trust · Risk assessment · Digital sovereignty

06

AI-Assisted Cybersecurity

AI and machine learning for intrusion detection, federated learning, adaptive decision support, anomaly detection, threat analysis, and trustworthy security automation.

AI · Federated learning · Intrusion detection · Decision support
