About

Research, teaching, and leadership in cybersecurity

I am an Assistant Professor of Cybersecurity at Linköping University, Sweden, and a part-time researcher at Kaunas University of Technology, Lithuania. My work spans applied cryptography, post-quantum security, aviation cybersecurity, industrial and IoT security, cyber resilience, and secure communication infrastructures.

I contribute to European and Nordic research initiatives addressing critical infrastructure protection, quantum readiness, digital sovereignty, cybersecurity education, and trustworthy emerging technologies. My research combines rigorous security analysis with practical validation and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Teaching & supervision

Connecting cybersecurity theory with professional practice

I teach cybersecurity, information security, cryptography, computer networks, and network security across undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional education. My teaching emphasises active learning, practical security analysis, and the responsible application of cybersecurity methods.

I supervise bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral research in collaboration with academic, public-sector, and industrial partners.
