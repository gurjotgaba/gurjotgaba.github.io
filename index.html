Areas of focus

Security challenges across emerging communication systems, critical infrastructures, and connected digital environments.

01

Applied Cryptography

Authentication, key establishment, privacy-preserving protocols, and secure communications.

02

Post-Quantum Security

Quantum readiness, crypto-agility, post-quantum migration, QKD, and EuroQCI.

03

Aviation Cybersecurity

Security of CPDLC, LDACS, future aviation networks, and air traffic management.

04

Industrial & IoT Security

Industry 4.0 and 5.0, IT/OT convergence, cyber-physical systems, IoT, and IIoT.

05

Cyber Resilience

Critical infrastructure protection, risk assessment, digital sovereignty, and resilience.

06

AI-Assisted Cybersecurity

AI-supported detection, federated learning, adaptive security, and trustworthy decision support.
