Research beyond the desk

International collaboration · Tallinn, Estonia · 2026

ENERGYCOM Network Meeting

Participated in the ENERGYCOM network meeting at TalTech, exchanging Nordic–Baltic practices for responsible AI integration in engineering education and strengthening collaboration across ENERGYCOM, KTU, IEEE Sweden, and IEEE Estonia.

European project · Brussels, Belgium · 2026

Digital4Business Interim Review

Represented Linköping University at the Digital4Business consortium meeting and European Commission interim review, contributing to progress assessment and planning for advanced digital-skills education.

Teaching and outreach · Linköping, Sweden · 2025

LiU Capture the Flag 2025

Led a cybersecurity competition involving 106 participants across 32 teams and 142 total participants, volunteers, and guests, connecting high-school students, university students, researchers, and industry professionals.

Leadership and training · Brussels, Belgium · 2025

COST Academy Young Researchers Training

Represented COST Action BEiNG-WISE at a leadership and career-development programme bringing together early-career researchers from approximately 30 COST Actions.

Research community · Stockholm, Sweden · 2025

SWITS 2025

Participated in the Swedish IT Security Network meeting at Cybercampus Sweden, together with researchers from 19 Swedish universities and the full AEGIS research group.

Award and conference · Brussels, Belgium · 2025

ICNS 2025 — Best of Session Award

Presented SEC-AIRSPACE research on SecRAM catalogues, received the Best of Session Award, and served as a session chair for the first time at EUROCONTROL Headquarters.

Research presentation · Bologna, Italy · 2025

Digital4Business Consortium Meeting

Presented work on the Cybersecurity for Business module at the University of Bologna and contributed to project planning and European collaboration in digital education.

International research collaboration · Dublin, Ireland · 2025

COST Action Meeting at UCD

Presented cybersecurity research at University College Dublin, explored collaboration opportunities, and visited Trinity College Dublin during the BEiNG-WISE meeting.

Field validation · Malmö, Sweden · 2025

SEC-AIRSPACE Validation Exercise

Conducted a five-day validation exercise at the LFV Air Traffic Control Centre with eight professional air traffic controllers, realistic cyber-attack scenarios, workload assessment, and physiological monitoring.
