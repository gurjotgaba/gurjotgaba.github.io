Biography

Building secure and resilient digital systems

I am an Assistant Professor of Cybersecurity at Linköping University, Sweden, and a part-time researcher at Kaunas University of Technology, Lithuania. My research focuses on applied cryptography, post-quantum security, aviation cybersecurity, industrial and IoT security, cyber resilience, and secure communication infrastructures.

My work combines protocol design, threat modelling, cyber risk assessment, simulation-based validation, and interdisciplinary collaboration. I contribute to European and Nordic initiatives addressing quantum readiness, digital sovereignty, critical infrastructure protection, cybersecurity education, and trustworthy emerging technologies.

I teach cybersecurity and information security, supervise student research, support international project development, and contribute to professional communities through IEEE, COST Actions, conference organisation, editorial work, and peer review.
